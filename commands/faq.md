Print the answers to these frequently asked questions. Display them clearly, formatted with bold question headers.

**Do I need a Claude subscription?**
Yes. Claude Pro ($20/mo) or Max ($100/mo). The free tier doesn't have enough capacity.

**Do I need to be technical?**
No. You talk to Dean in plain English. The setup wizard handles everything.

**What if setup gets interrupted?**
Run `/full-onboarding` again. It picks up at the first unfinished file and never re-asks what's done.

**Can I redo setup?**
Yes. Run `/full-onboarding` anytime. It detects your existing setup and never overwrites a file you've confirmed.

**Does it send emails or post automatically?**
No. Everything is a draft. Nothing sends without your approval.

**Does it remember things between sessions?**
Yes. Corrections become preferences. Outcomes get logged. Each session builds on the last.

**Can I add my own skills?**
Yes. Say "Build me a skill for [describe your workflow]." Custom skills live in your workspace and are never affected by plugin updates.

**What if the voice is wrong?**
Say "that's too formal" or "make it warmer." Corrections get saved to your voice profile.

**How do I upgrade?**
Download the new ZIP, re-install in Cowork. Skills update. Workspace files untouched.

**How do I edit my system?**
All workspace files are yours. Edit `shared/voice.md` for how you sound, `shared/audience.md` for who you serve, `shared/offers.md` for what you sell, `shared/goals.md` for your goals, and a department (or client/project) folder for how that part of the business behaves. Or just tell Dean what to change - Dean updates the files for you.

**Where do outputs go?**
`outputs/YYYY-MM/` organized by type - emails/, social/, lessons/, plans/, reports/, comms/.

**Support:** Check your product listing or purchase receipt for the current support channel.
