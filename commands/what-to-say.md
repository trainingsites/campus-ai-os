description: "Example prompts for your AI team"
---

Print this list of example prompts the owner can use with their AI team. Display them clearly, organized by category.

## Starting Your Day
- "What should I work on today?"
- "Morning brief"
- "What's the status across the team?"
- "What's waiting on me?"

## Content
- "Repurpose this transcript into social posts" (paste or drop a transcript)
- "Plan my social media for next week"
- "Write a blog post about [topic]"
- "Build a lesson plan for [topic]"
- "Create a course outline for [topic]"
- "Plan my next session with [client name]"
- "Turn this recording into content" (paste a transcript or summary)

## Marketing
- "Write a welcome email sequence for new [students/clients]"
- "Build a launch plan for [offer name]"
- "Create a 4-email nurture sequence about [topic]"
- "Design an offer for [what you want to sell]"
- "What content opportunities should I be looking at?"
- "Write a re-engagement email for people who haven't opened in 30 days"

## Client / Student Work
- "Draft a follow-up email for [client name]"
- "Write a check-in message for my students"
- "What should I cover in my next session with [client]?"
- "Create a welcome packet for new [clients/students]"

## Planning
- "Plan my week"
- "Review my system — what's working and what needs attention?"
- "I want to launch [offer] — what do we need to do?"
- "What did my team deliver this month?"

## Building
- "Build me a custom skill for [workflow you do repeatedly]"
- "I want to add a new capability for [describe what you need]"

## Tips
- **You don't need slash commands.** Plain language works for everything.
- **Paste things in.** Transcripts, emails, notes, URLs — your team works with whatever you give them.
- **Correct them.** If output doesn't sound like you, say so. Corrections get saved.
- **Everything is a draft.** Nothing sends or publishes without your approval.
