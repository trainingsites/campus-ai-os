Run the full onboarding: a proper sit-down where your chief of staff interviews you about your business, who you serve, what you sell, your goals, your tools, and how you sound - and turns each answer into a real working file. This is the default at first run, and you can run it any time after. It's resumable and non-destructive: on a campus that's already partly set up it picks up at the first file that isn't done and never re-asks what you've already drafted or confirmed. Ends by producing your first real piece of work on the richer context.
