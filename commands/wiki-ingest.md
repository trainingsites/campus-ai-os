description: "Add source material to your business wiki - extracts what was learned"
argument-hint: "[file path, pasted text, or 'this session']"
---

Take source material — a transcript, article, meeting notes, research, or pasted text — and add it to your business wiki. Your team reads the source, extracts what was LEARNED (not just what happened), and updates or creates the right pages across the six wiki categories: clients, concepts, offers, decisions, market, operations. The index and log are updated automatically. Updates beat creates — if a page exists for the topic, it gets refined rather than duplicated.
