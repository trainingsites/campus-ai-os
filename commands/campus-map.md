Generate the Campus Map — a self-contained, file-based command-center dashboard for this campus. Dean reads the campus's own files (goals, activity ledger, wiki, outputs, scheduled tasks, briefs, and team results) and bakes a portable `campus-map.html` you double-click in any browser. No server, no database, no connectors. The map is private-only. Re-run any time to refresh as your campus does more work.
